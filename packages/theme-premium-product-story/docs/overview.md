# Theme Premium Product Story

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What this theme gives you

A polished look for consumer product storytelling. It gives your site an expansive, launch-led direction tuned for product families.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Premium Product Story** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Expansive launches, product families, and feature highlights.
- Gallery strips, ecosystem modules, and comparisons.
- Purchase call-to-actions with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with your commerce setup for live product listings.
