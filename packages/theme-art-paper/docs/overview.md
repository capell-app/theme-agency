# Theme Art Paper

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What this theme gives you

A photography-led look for design magazines. It gives your site a visual, gallery-first editorial direction.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Art Paper** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Photography-led lead stories and editor picks.
- Vertical categories, gallery features, and product credits.
- Trend lists, archives, and newsletter conversion with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with Blog for your articles.
