## What it does for you

Contacts keeps the people you deal with in one place. You add and import contacts, group them with tags, merge duplicates, and export a list when you need it.

## Your screens

- **Contacts**: your list of people.
- **Contact overview**: a single contact's details and activity.
- **Tags**: the groups you use to segment contacts.

## What you can do

- Add a contact.
- Import contacts from a spreadsheet.
- Group contacts with tags.
- Merge duplicate contacts.
- Export a list.

## Where to find it

Go to **Contacts** in the admin to add, import, and segment contacts.

## Good to know

- Keep your **contacts** in one place instead of scattered spreadsheets.
- Group them with **tags** so you can find a segment quickly.
- Respect the consent you collected when contacting people.
