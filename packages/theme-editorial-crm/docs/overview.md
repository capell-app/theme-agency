# Theme Editorial CRM

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What this theme gives you

A clean, product-led look for CRM and business software. It gives your site a neutral, professional direction tuned for product and dashboard storytelling.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Editorial CRM** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Product dashboards, data models, and workflow automation sections.
- Collaboration, integrations, and reporting modules.
- Customer stories and flexible dashboards with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Your text and images stay yours; the theme only changes the design.
