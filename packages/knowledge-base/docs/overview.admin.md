## What it does for you

Knowledge Base lets you publish help articles that your visitors can browse and search. You write articles, group them into collections, and publish them when they are ready for customers. It is the place for how-to guides, FAQs, and support content.

## Your screens

- **Articles**: your help articles and their status.
- **Collections**: the groups that organise your articles.
- **The article editor**: where you write an article and set whether it is public.

## What you can do

- Create a help article.
- Create a collection to group articles.
- Organise articles into collections.
- Publish an article when it is ready.
- Let visitors search the knowledge base on your site.

## Where to find it

Go to **Knowledge Base** in the admin. Use **Create article** and **Create collection** to add content.

## Good to know

- Create help articles your visitors can search.
- Group articles into **collections** so they are easy to browse.
- An article is only visible to visitors once it is **Public** and published.
