## What this theme gives you

A restrained, elegant look for fashion retail. It gives your store a sparse, lookbook-led direction tuned for seasonal collections.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Minimal Fashion** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Seasonal collections, sparse category paths, and lookbooks.
- Product care, materials, and store assistance sections.
- Newsletter conversion with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with your commerce setup for live product listings.
