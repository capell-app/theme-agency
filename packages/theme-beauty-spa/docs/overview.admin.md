## What this theme gives you

A calm, inviting look for day spas and beauty clinics. It gives your site a relaxing, premium direction tuned for treatments and bookings.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Beauty & Spa** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Page styles suited to a spa or beauty clinic site.
- Portable demo content to start from.
- Colours and fonts tuned for the beauty and spa look, all editable in Theme settings.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with Bookings if you take appointments online.
