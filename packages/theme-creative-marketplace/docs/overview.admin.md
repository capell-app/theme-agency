## What this theme gives you

A bright, energetic look for a creative services marketplace. It gives your site a vibrant direction tuned for browsing makers and services.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Creative Marketplace** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Service heroes, category chips, and shot grids.
- Profiles, service categories, briefs, pricing snippets, and availability.
- Search and hiring call-to-action sections with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Your text and images stay yours; the theme only changes the design.
