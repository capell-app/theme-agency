# Newsletter

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Newsletter lets you build and send email newsletters and manage who is subscribed. You write a newsletter, send a test to yourself, schedule the real send, and see how many people opened it.

## Your screens

- **Newsletter sends**: your newsletters and their status.
- **Subscribers**: who has subscribed.
- **Provider connections**: the email service used to send.

## What you can do

- Write and send a newsletter.
- Send a test to yourself first.
- Manage who is subscribed.
- Schedule a send for later.
- See how many people opened a newsletter.

## Where to find it

Go to **Newsletter** in the admin to write sends and manage subscribers.

## Good to know

- Write your newsletter and **Send a test** to yourself before sending to everyone.
- Manage who is subscribed, and respect the consent you collected.
- Schedule the send for a good time, such as the morning.
