# Frontend Optimizer

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Frontend Optimizer makes your pages load faster automatically by streamlining the files visitors download. Most of the time there is nothing to do; you may occasionally clear the optimised files after a big change.

## Your screens

- **Optimizer settings**: the speed-up options you can turn on or off.

## What you can do

- Let pages load faster automatically.
- Turn safe optimisations on or off.
- Clear optimised files after a big change.

## Where to find it

Go to **Frontend Optimizer** in the admin for its settings.

## Good to know

- Makes pages load faster automatically, with little for you to do.
- Clear optimised files after a big change if something looks off.
- Stick to the safe toggles unless your developer advises otherwise.
