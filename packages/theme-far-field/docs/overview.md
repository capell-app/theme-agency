# Theme Far Field

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What this theme gives you

A premium, global editorial look for culture and lifestyle publishing. It gives your site a rich magazine direction tuned for dispatches and guides.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Far Field** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Lead dispatches, audio rows, and city guides.
- Travel, culture, books, and columnist sections.
- Archives and newsletter conversion with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with Blog for your articles.
