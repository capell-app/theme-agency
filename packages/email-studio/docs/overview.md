# Email Studio

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Email Studio is where you brand and edit the emails your site sends, like welcome and password-reset messages. You edit the template, preview it, send a test, and check the log to confirm an email went out and was opened.

## Your screens

- **Email templates**: the emails your site sends, ready to edit and brand.
- **Preview**: how a template looks before it goes live.
- **Message log**: a record of emails sent, including opens.

## What you can do

- Edit and brand an email template.
- Preview a template.
- Send a test before going live.
- Check whether an email was sent and opened.

## Where to find it

Go to **Email Studio** in the admin to edit templates and review the message log.

## Good to know

- Edit the email template your customers receive, so it matches your brand.
- **Send a test** before going live.
- Check the **message log** if an email didn't arrive, and see whether it was **Opened**.
