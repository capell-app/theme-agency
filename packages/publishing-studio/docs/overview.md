# Publishing Studio

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Publishing Studio gives your team a safe way to change live content. You make edits in a private release workspace, send them for review, and only publish once they are approved. You can schedule a release for a future date, share a preview link before anything goes live, and roll back to an earlier version in seconds if something needs correcting.

## Your screens

- **Release workspace**: a private copy of your content where you make and stage changes.
- **Approvals & change requests**: where reviewers approve work or ask for changes.
- **Content Scheduler**: where you set when an approved release goes live.
- **Preview links**: shareable previews of a workspace before it publishes.
- **Rollback-ready versions**: earlier published versions you can restore.

## What you can do

- Create a release workspace and make your edits privately.
- Submit a workspace for review.
- Approve a release as a reviewer.
- Schedule an approved release for a future date.
- Share a preview link.
- Roll back a release to an earlier version.

## Where to find it

Open **Publishing Studio** in the admin. Start in a release workspace, then use **Approvals & change requests**, the **Content Scheduler**, and **Rollback-ready versions** as the work moves through review to publish.

## Good to know

- Your changes stay in a private workspace until they are approved. Visitors never see unapproved work.
- A workspace must be approved before it can be scheduled to publish.
- Use a **Preview link** to show work to others without publishing it.
- Use **Roll back** to undo a release in seconds.
