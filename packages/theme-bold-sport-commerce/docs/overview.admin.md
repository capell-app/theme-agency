## What this theme gives you

A high-energy look for sport retail. It gives your store campaign launches, product walls, and drop-alert energy tuned to convert.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Bold Sport Commerce** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Campaign launches, category jumps, product walls, fit details, and training stories.
- Community offers and drop-alert conversion sections.
- Portable demo content to start from.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with your commerce setup for live product listings.
