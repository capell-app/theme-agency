## What this theme gives you

A focused one-page showcase look. It gives your site a single-scroll direction for templates, tools, and showcases.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **One Take** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- A showcase hero, category tabs, and one-page grids.
- Templates, sections, tools, and sponsors.
- Screenshot cards and submit call-to-actions with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Your text and images stay yours; the theme only changes the design.
