# Using GA4 Reports

This guide is for owners and operators who read traffic numbers. Every step uses the labels you see on screen.

## What you're looking at

| Screen or signal | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **GA4 Reports overview** | Your traffic and key metrics |
| A **Metric** | One measure, such as visitors or page views |
| **Sync status** | Whether the connection to Google Analytics is healthy |

## What to do when...

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| No numbers appear | GA4 isn't connected; connect your Google Analytics |
| Sync shows failed | Check the **Last error** and re-check the connection with your developer |
| A metric is unfamiliar | Note what it measures; for example "bounce rate" is the share of visitors who leave after one page |
| Numbers look out of date | Wait for the next sync, or check **sync status** |

## Settings and retention

- Turn on **Enable GA4 Reports reporting** and connect your analytics account.
- Numbers sync from Google Analytics on a schedule; you don't enter them by hand.
