## What this theme gives you

A polished, credibility-first look for established businesses, advisory firms, and public bodies. Six presets (Boardroom, Civic, Advisory, Integrity, Enterprise Trust, Public Ledger) span formal navy through accessible civic and editorial advisory looks, with full dark mode.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Theme Corporate** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts, and pick a preset.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Proof carousels, capability bands, and a measured content hierarchy.
- Six presets that restyle the whole site with no code.
- Portable demo content to start from.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- Pick a preset in **Theme settings** to change the whole feel at once.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
