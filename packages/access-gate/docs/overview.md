# Access Gate

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Access Gate puts a wall in front of pages, downloads, or member areas. Visitors ask for access, you approve or reject them, and only approved people get through. Every request, approval, and visit is recorded so you can see who has access and take it away when needed.

## Your screens

- **Access areas**: the protected areas you have set up and how each one lets people in.
- **Registrations**: incoming access requests waiting for review.
- **Grants**: who currently has access.
- **Claim links**: the approval links sent to people, and whether they were used.
- **Browser sessions**: device-bound access you can revoke.
- **Audit events**: the full history of requests, approvals, and blocked visits.

## What you can do

- Set up a protected area and choose how people get in.
- Approve, reject, or expire access requests.
- See who has access right now and revoke it.
- Resend an approval link when someone loses theirs.
- Look back over the history of any access request.

## Where to find it

Everything lives under the **Access Gate** group in the admin sidebar.

## Good to know

- Visitors only ever see a request form or a blocked message. They never see admin labels or internal details.
- Revoking a **grant** takes access away immediately.
- The **Audit events** screen is your record of what happened and when.
