## What this theme gives you

A product-company journal look with dark mastheads. It gives your site an editorial direction for product updates and engineering posts.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Product Company Editorial** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Topic labels, author metadata, and product updates.
- Design essays, engineering posts, and templates.
- Culture stories and newsletter conversion with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with Blog for your articles.
