# Frontend Authoring

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Frontend Authoring lets you edit a page directly on the live site. When you are signed in as an admin, editable areas appear on the page; you click one, change the content where you see it, and save. It is best for quick, in-context edits.

## Your screens

- **The live page in edit mode**: the page itself, with editable areas highlighted.
- **The inline editor**: the panel that opens when you click an editable area.

## What you can do

- Edit a page from the live site.
- See which areas of a page are editable.
- Change content in place and save.

## Where to find it

Sign in as an admin, open the page on your site, and click **Edit** to turn on edit mode.

## Good to know

- Log in, then click **Edit** right on the page to see the editable areas.
- Change the text where you see it, instead of hunting through the admin.
- Edits save in place; clear the page cache if a change doesn't show immediately.
