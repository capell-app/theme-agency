## What it does for you

Shopify Commerce connects your Shopify store to Capell so your products appear in admin search and stay in sync. You connect the store, let products sync, search the catalogue, and re-sync if something looks out of date.

## Your screens

- **Connection**: your connected Shopify store and its sync status.
- **Products**: synced products, searchable in admin.

## What you can do

- Connect your Shopify store.
- Sync products into Capell.
- Search the catalogue in admin.
- Re-sync when a product looks out of date.

## Where to find it

Go to **Shopify Commerce** in the admin to connect your store and check sync status.

## Good to know

- **Connect** your Shopify store before relying on the catalogue.
- Synced products appear in admin search.
- Re-sync if a product looks out of date; the screen shows the **Last sync** time.
