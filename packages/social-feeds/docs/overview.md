# Social Feeds

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Social Feeds shows your latest social posts on your site. You connect a feed, choose how many posts appear, embed it on a page, and reconnect if it stops updating.

## Your screens

- **Social feed connections**: the social accounts you have connected.
- **The feed widget**: the block that shows posts on a page.

## What you can do

- Connect a social feed.
- Choose how many posts show.
- Embed the feed on a page.
- Reconnect a feed that stopped updating.

## Where to find it

Go to **Social Feeds** in the admin to manage connections.

## Good to know

- Show your latest posts on the site to keep it fresh.
- Choose how many posts appear so the feed doesn't take over the page.
- Reconnect if the feed stops updating (social networks limit how often feeds refresh).
