## What this theme gives you

A calm, daily-curation look with clean white pages and small, tidy labels. It gives your site a minimal feed direction for curated picks.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Minimal Curation Feed** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Loose masonry columns and screenshot cards.
- Category tabs, search, and live update indicators.
- Best-of lists, latest feeds, and a newsletter signup with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Your text and images stay yours; the theme only changes the design.
