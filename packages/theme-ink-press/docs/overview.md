# Theme Ink Press

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What this theme gives you

A serious newsroom look for news and analysis sites. It gives your site a dense, information-rich direction tuned for top stories and live coverage.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Ink Press** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Top stories, live briefs, and topic navigation.
- Opinion, video, missed-it recaps, and archives.
- Newsletter inserts and portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Pairs well with Blog for your articles.
