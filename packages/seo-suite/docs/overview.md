# SEO Suite

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

SEO Suite helps your pages get found. It audits a page for missing or weak SEO details, drafts titles and descriptions for you with AI Creator, helps you turn broken links into redirects, and shows how your pages are doing in search.

## Your screens

- **SEO audit**: the check that lists what a page is missing for search.
- **AI Creator**: the assistant that drafts a page title and meta description.
- **Broken links and 404s**: pages visitors couldn't find, ready to redirect.
- **Rankings**: how your pages perform in search over time.

## What you can do

- Run an SEO audit on a page and read the findings.
- Use AI Creator to draft a title and meta description.
- Turn a broken link or 404 into a redirect.
- Review broken links across your site.
- Track how pages rank in search.

## Where to find it

Open **SEO Suite** in the admin. The SEO audit and AI Creator also appear while you edit a page.

## Good to know

- Run an **SEO audit** to see what is missing before a page goes live.
- Use **AI Creator** to draft a title and meta description, then edit it to sound like you.
- Turn a **404** into a redirect so visitors land in the right place instead of an error.
