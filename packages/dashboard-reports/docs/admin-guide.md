# Using Dashboard Reports

This guide is for owners and operators who read the dashboard. Every step uses the labels you see on screen.

## What you're looking at

| Screen or signal | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard cards | Your key numbers, such as published and scheduled pages |
| **Content health** | Whether your content has issues to fix |
| A digest email | The same numbers sent to you on a schedule |

## What to do when...

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The dashboard is cluttered | Turn off cards you don't use in the card settings |
| A number looks wrong | Open the card's detail to see what it counts |
| You want the numbers elsewhere | Export the report |
| Content health flags issues | Open the linked content and fix what's flagged |

## Settings and retention

- Choose which **cards** appear so the dashboard stays focused.
- A digest can send the numbers to you on a schedule, so you don't have to check manually.
