## What it does for you

Dashboard Reports puts your key numbers on the admin dashboard, like published and scheduled pages and content health. You read the cards at a glance and choose which ones appear.

## Your screens

- **The dashboard**: the cards showing your key numbers.
- **Card settings**: which cards appear.

## What you can do

- See your key numbers at a glance.
- Choose which cards appear.
- Export a report.

## Where to find it

The cards appear on your admin **dashboard**.

## Good to know

- Your key numbers at a glance, without digging through screens.
- Choose which **cards** appear so the dashboard shows what matters to you.
- You can export the underlying numbers when you need a record.
