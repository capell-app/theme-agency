## What this theme gives you

A design-awards scoreboard look with warm off-white pages and numeric scores. It gives your site a recognition-led direction for judging and winners.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Scoreboard Showcase** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Numeric scores, segmented judging criteria, and winner-of-the-day modules.
- Newest nominees, previous winners, and public voting states.
- Score breakdowns, creator credits, and archive pages with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Your text and images stay yours; the theme only changes the design.
