# Diagnostics

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Diagnostics gives you a system health screen that checks the parts of your site and tells you what is healthy and what needs attention. It is a quick way to see whether something is wrong and whether you can fix it or need a developer.

## Your screens

- **System health**: the overall checks and their status.
- **Cache health**, **queue health**, and **content graph**: specific areas being checked.

## What you can do

- See whether your site's systems are healthy.
- Read what each check found.
- Tell the difference between things you can fix and things for a developer.

## Where to find it

Open **System health** in the admin to see the checks.

## Good to know

- Green means healthy; a warning or failure means something needs attention.
- Some items you can act on yourself; others you should send to your developer.
- It is a read-only health view, so checking it can't break anything.
