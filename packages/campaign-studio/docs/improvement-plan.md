# Campaign Studio — Improvement & Growth Plan

> Package: capell-app/campaign-studio · Kind: package · Tier: premium · Product group: Capell Growth · Bundle: growth · Status: Draft

## 1. Snapshot

Campaign Studio is a schema-owning growth package (surfaces `admin` + `frontend`) that adds campaign groups, landing-page variants, UTM attribution, CTA/hero/lead-form widgets, conversion goals, and funnel/overview reporting on top of Capell. It owns five tables (`campaign_groups`, `campaign_landing_pages`, `campaign_cta_widgets`, `campaign_conversion_goals`, `campaign_conversions`) and five models, drives four Filament resources plus three dashboard contributions, and exposes 16 Actions (recording, attribution, variant resolution, URL building, stats, experiment sync). It hard-requires `admin`, `core`, `form-builder`, `frontend`, `insights`, `layout-builder` and softly supports `experiments` + `seo-suite`/`site-discovery`. Conversion capture today flows almost entirely through the FormBuilder `FormSubmitted` listener (`src/Listeners/RecordFormSubmissionConversion.php`); CTA-click and page-view recording Actions exist but have **no production caller**.

Current marketplace summary (verbatim): _"Campaign Studio adds campaign groups, landing-page variants, audience targeting, experiments, CTA widgets, conversion goals, UTM attribution, funnel reporting, and conversion reporting to Capell."_ Screenshots in `capell.json` marketplace block: **1** (`docs/assets/marketplace/extension-card.jpg`). Note `docs/screenshots/` actually contains 14 light/dark PNGs that are not referenced by the manifest.

## 2. Improvements (existing functionality)

- **Wire CTA-click conversions to a real capture path.** `RecordCtaClickConversionAction` and `RecordPageViewConversionAction` are fully implemented and unit-tested but are never invoked outside tests (only `RecordFormSubmissionConversion` is wired via `Event::listen`). The Blade widgets emit `data-campaign-goal` / `data-campaign-cta` attributes that nothing consumes. This means two of the four `ConversionGoalType` cases (`cta_click`, `page_view`) cannot record in production. Add a beacon endpoint or Insights-event subscriber that resolves these Actions. — restores advertised capability — `src/Actions/RecordCtaClickConversionAction.php`, `src/Actions/RecordPageViewConversionAction.php`, `src/Providers/CampaignStudioServiceProvider.php::registerListeners()` — L
- **Resolve or remove the `AttributionModel` enum.** `src/Enums/AttributionModel.php` (FirstTouch/LastTouch) is dead code — grepped across `src/` it appears only in its own definition. `BuildConversionAttributionAction` hardcodes both first- and last-touch fields onto every `ConversionAttributionData` row and never branches on a model. Either make goals/groups carry an `AttributionModel` that the action honours, or delete the enum. — removes misleading API + clarifies attribution semantics — `src/Enums/AttributionModel.php`, `src/Actions/BuildConversionAttributionAction.php` — M
- **Make `BuildCampaignOverviewStatsAction` conversion-rate join robust.** The visit count joins `campaign_groups.utm_campaign` to `insights_visits.utm_campaign` (`src/Actions/BuildCampaignOverviewStatsAction.php` L46-52). Groups with a null/blank `utm_campaign` silently contribute zero visits, inflating or zeroing the rate; multiple groups sharing a `utm_campaign` double-count. Document the constraint and guard against null `utm_campaign` joins. — correctness of a headline KPI — `src/Actions/BuildCampaignOverviewStatsAction.php` — M
- **Variant resolution ignores active/published state of the target page.** `ResolveCampaignLandingPageVariantAction` selects by `is_primary` and UTM columns only; it never checks the linked `Page` status, so a draft/unpublished page can be chosen as a variant. Filter to published pages or document that callers must. — prevents serving unpublished content — `src/Actions/ResolveCampaignLandingPageVariantAction.php` — M
- **Eager-load to avoid N+1 in funnel + landing-page queries.** `BuildCampaignConversionFunnelAction` is clean (`withCount`), but `CampaignLandingPagePublicUrlContributor::publicUrls()` loads all landing pages with nested `page.pageUrls.*` and then `unique()`s in PHP — fine for sitemaps but unbounded as campaigns grow. Add chunking/pagination for large sites. — sitemap-build scalability — `src/Support/PublicUrls/CampaignLandingPagePublicUrlContributor.php` — S
- **Hero/lead-form button URLs bypass `BuildCampaignUrlAction`.** Only the CTA widget appends UTMs to action URLs (`campaign-cta-widget.blade.php`). The hero (`campaign-hero.blade.php`) and lead-form CTAs render raw `primary_button_url` with no UTM decoration, so clicks from those widgets lose campaign attribution. Route those URLs through `BuildCampaignUrlAction` with the group's default UTM. — consistent attribution across all campaign widgets — `resources/views/components/widget/campaign-hero.blade.php` — S
- **Surface the committed screenshots in the manifest.** 14 light/dark PNGs exist under `docs/screenshots/` and are listed in `docs/overview.md`, but `capell.json` `marketplace.screenshots` references only the single `extension-card.jpg`. Promote the real product screenshots into the manifest. — marketplace listing quality — `capell.json` — S
- **Populate the changelog.** `CHANGELOG.md` has a single placeholder line ("Prepared package metadata…"). For a premium first-party package this should track schema/Action changes. — release hygiene / buyer trust — `CHANGELOG.md` — S

## 3. Missing Features (gaps)

Mapped against declared `capabilities[]` (`campaign-audience-targeting`, `campaign-conversion-funnel-reporting`, `landing-page-variant-experiments`, `campaign-experiment-sync`, …) and standard campaign-marketing norms.

- **Client-side conversion capture (table stakes).** There is no JS, no beacon route, and no Livewire action that turns a CTA/hero click into a `campaign_conversions` row. `capell.json` declares `campaign-studio-frontend` and the overview says "No explicit public route is registered." Today only form submissions convert. A small tracking script + signed endpoint (consuming the already-emitted `data-campaign-*` attributes) is the missing keystone. — **table stakes**.
- **Audience targeting beyond UTM (differentiator vs gap).** `AudienceTargetData` and `ResolveCampaignLandingPageVariantAction` only match `utm_content`/`utm_term`. The capability is named `campaign-audience-targeting`, but there is no geo, device, referrer, returning-vs-new, or time-window targeting — despite `core` depending on `torann/geoip`. Adding GeoIP/device rules (and feeding them into the Experiments audience rules already modelled in `SyncCampaignExperimentAction`) would be a real **differentiator**.
- **A/B test result readout in-package (table stakes for "variant-experiments").** `SyncCampaignExperimentAction` pushes variants/goals into the Experiments package, but Campaign Studio has no admin view that shows per-variant conversion rates or statistical lift back inside the campaign. Buyers expect to see which variant won without leaving the campaign screen. — **table stakes**.
- **Scheduling automation.** `CampaignGroup` has `starts_at`/`ends_at` and a `Scheduled` status, plus an editorial-calendar contributor, but nothing transitions a `Scheduled` campaign to `Active` (or `Active`→`Ended`) when the window opens/closes. There is no scheduled command. — table stakes for "campaign scheduling".
- **Multichannel / channel taxonomy.** Attribution is UTM-only. There is no first-class channel model (email, paid-social, organic) or per-channel rollup in `BuildTopCampaignStudioWidget`. Marketing buyers expect channel breakdowns. — differentiator.
- **Revenue / value attribution.** `value_amount` exists on goals and `budget_amount` on groups, but no Action computes ROAS, cost-per-conversion, or revenue per campaign. The funnel only counts conversions. Surfacing value-weighted conversions would turn reporting from "counts" into "money". — differentiator.
- **Conversion-window / dedup configurability.** The dedup unique key (`campaign_conversion_goal_id, insights_visit_id, insights_event_id, source_type, source_id`) is hardcoded in migration 05; there is no attribution lookback window (e.g. 7/30-day). Conversions with no visit/event/source skip dedup entirely (`hasIdentity()` returns false → plain `create`). — table stakes.
- **Public landing-page route ownership.** The package contributes URLs to Site Discovery but renders through `core`'s `capell.pages.show`. There is no campaign-native short URL / vanity slug (`/go/{campaign}`) that auto-applies UTMs and redirects — a common campaign-tool feature. — differentiator.

## 4. Issues / Risks

- **Cache-vs-personalization contradiction.** Manifest sets `performance.cacheSafety.cacheable: false`, `variesBy: ["site","locale"]`, and `frontendRenderBudgetMs: 20`. But the frontend middleware chain (`frontend.cache`) serves static HTML files for anonymous users, and campaign widgets render inside ordinary pages. Variant selection / UTM-aware personalization cannot take effect once a page is statically cached, and `variesBy` does not include UTM params. Either the widgets must render uncached fragments (Livewire/ESI) or the personalization promise is unenforceable. No test asserts cached-vs-personalized behaviour. — `capell.json` (performance block), `resources/views/components/widget/*.blade.php` — **High**.
- **Dead enum ships in a premium API.** `AttributionModel` (Section 2) is unused; for a `proposedLicense: paid` / `first-party` package this is API debt buyers may build against. — `src/Enums/AttributionModel.php` — Medium.
- **CTA/page-view Actions are untested _in integration_ and unreachable in production.** Unit tests cover them, but with no caller the advertised CTA/page-view conversion capability is effectively absent (Section 3). — Medium.
- **No frontend render/feature test.** Coverage (38+ tests) is strong on Actions, Data mapping, Filament site-scoping, and policies, and there is a good `it('renders public CTA tracking attributes without leaking numeric campaign ids')` unit test. But nothing boots an actual page through the frontend middleware to prove the widgets render, that no admin markers/IDs leak in a full page, or that lead-form embeds resolve. The capell skill requires rendering/cache changes to have anonymous + non-admin safety tests. — `tests/` (no `Feature/` dir) — Medium.
- **Public-output safety: currently OK, keep it that way.** `tracking/attributes.blade.php` and `campaign-cta-widget.blade.php` expose only `campaignGroup->slug` and goal/cta `key` — no numeric IDs, field paths, or model names — and a test guards this. The hero/lead-form widgets render `data-campaign-goal` from `getMeta('goal_key')` (admin-authored string), which is acceptable. Risk is regression if a future change emits IDs; lock with a render-level (not just component-level) assertion. — `resources/views/components/**` — Low (but easy to regress).
- **`SyncCampaignExperimentAction` uses `forceFill(...)->save()` and direct relation writes.** It bypasses the Experiments package's own create Action on the update path (only the create path calls `CreateExperimentAction`). If Experiments adds validation/events to variant/goal writes, this drifts. Cross-package write coupling is a maintenance risk. — `src/Actions/SyncCampaignExperimentAction.php` L48-65, L199-250 — Medium.
- **`utm_campaign` join has no uniqueness guarantee.** Multiple campaign groups can share a `utm_campaign` (only `slug` is unique). The overview/visit join (Section 2) will mis-attribute. — `database/migrations/...01_create_campaign_groups_table.php`, `src/Actions/BuildCampaignOverviewStatsAction.php` — Medium.
- **i18n completeness.** Enum labels and Filament strings are translated via `__()` (good). Verify all five lang files (`form`, `generic`, `navigation`, `package`, `widgets`) carry every key the code references (e.g. `generic.landing_page_variant`, `widgets.active_campaign-studio`); the odd `active_campaign-studio` array key (hyphen in an array key/translation slug) is fragile. — `resources/lang/en/*`, `src/Actions/BuildCampaignOverviewStatsAction.php` — Low.
- **Performance budget unverified.** `adminQueryBudget: 40` and `frontendRenderBudgetMs: 20` are declared but no test or benchmark asserts them; the CTA hydrate batch (`CampaignCtaWidget::hydrateWidgets`) is well-designed for it, but unmeasured. — `capell.json` — Low.

## 5. Marketplace & Selling

**Critique of current copy.** The marketplace `summary` and the `capell.json` `description` are _identical_ and are a comma-spliced feature dump ("groups, landing-page variants, audience targeting, experiments, CTA widgets, conversion goals, UTM attribution, funnel reporting, and conversion reporting") — nine nouns, no benefit, no buyer. The composer `description` ("Campaign landing pages, CTA widgets, UTM attribution, conversion goals, and campaign insights.") is tighter but still a parts list and _inconsistent_ with the manifest (drops variants/experiments/funnel). Neither says who it's for or what outcome it drives.

**Improved one-sentence summary:**

> Launch, target, and measure marketing campaigns inside Capell — build landing-page variants, drop in CTA and lead-capture widgets, and track UTM-attributed conversions and funnels without bolting on a separate analytics tool.

**Improved 3–4 sentence description:**

> Campaign Studio turns Capell into a campaign command centre for marketing and growth teams. Group your landing pages, CTAs, and lead forms under a campaign, target visitors with UTM-driven page variants, and let conversions flow in automatically from form submissions and on-page goals. Built-in funnel and overview reporting show which campaigns and pages actually convert — and how that rate trends week over week — without exporting to a third-party tool. When the Experiments add-on is installed, your landing-page variants and conversion goals sync straight into a running A/B test.

**Screenshot / media gaps.** Manifest exposes only `extension-card.jpg` despite 14 real screenshots (light + dark for groups index, landing-pages index, conversion-goal form, CTA form, dashboard widgets, frontend landing page) sitting in `docs/screenshots/`. Promote 4–6 of these into `marketplace.screenshots` with captions. Missing entirely: a hero showing the funnel/overview stats, and an animated GIF of variant selection or the experiment sync. The dashboard-widgets screenshot is still flagged "keep as text" in `docs/overview.md` even though the PNG is committed — update that doc.

**Pricing / tier / bundle positioning.** Premium tier in the `growth` bundle is right — this is a paid, first-party, priority-support package. It is the natural _anchor_ of the Capell Growth bundle: it hard-requires `insights` (analytics), `form-builder` (lead capture), and `layout-builder` (widgets), so selling Campaign Studio pulls those in. Cross-sell levers: (1) `experiments` add-on as an upsell unlocked by the existing `SyncCampaignExperimentAction`; (2) `seo-suite` / `site-discovery` for sitemap + AI-discovery of landing pages (already wired via `CampaignLandingPagePublicUrlContributor`); (3) bundle with a future "Automation Studio" that listens to the already-dispatched `CampaignConverted` event. Position as the Extension Suite centrepiece: "Campaign Studio + Experiments + SEO Suite = the Capell Growth Suite."

**Differentiators / value props / target buyer.** Target buyer: the in-house marketer or agency running campaigns on a Capell-built site who today stitches together GA + a form tool + spreadsheets. Value props: campaigns, variants, widgets, and conversion reporting native to the CMS (no tag-manager glue); UTM attribution captured server-side and deduped; one-click sync to real A/B experiments; landing pages that automatically enter the sitemap and AI-discovery feeds. Differentiator to lean into: _closed-loop_ — the same campaign that builds the page also reports its conversions and feeds the experiment.

**Keywords / tags (8–12):** campaign management, landing pages, conversion tracking, UTM attribution, A/B testing, lead capture, marketing CMS, CTA widgets, conversion funnel, growth marketing, audience targeting, Filament.
