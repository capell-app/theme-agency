## What it does for you

Campaign Studio groups your marketing pages together and measures whether they work. You gather landing pages under a campaign, add a call-to-action, tell the dashboard what counts as a conversion, and watch how the campaign performs.

## Your screens

- **Campaign groups**: your campaigns and the pages in each.
- **Campaign overview**: the dashboard for a campaign's performance.
- **CTA, hero, and lead form widgets**: the campaign blocks you add to pages.
- **Conversion goals**: what you count as success.

## What you can do

- Create a campaign group and add pages to it.
- Add a call-to-action, hero, or lead form to a page.
- Set what counts as a conversion.
- Read the campaign dashboard.

## Where to find it

Go to **Campaign Studio** in the admin to build campaign groups and read their overview.

## Good to know

- Group pages under a campaign like "Summer Sale 2026" so you can measure them together.
- Add a **call to action** so visitors know what to do next.
- Tell the dashboard what counts as a **conversion**, or it can't measure success.
