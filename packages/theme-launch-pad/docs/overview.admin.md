## What this theme gives you

A polished landing-page gallery look with warm, refined styling. It gives your site a curated direction for showcasing pages and templates.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Launch Pad** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Rounded screenshot cards, latest sites, and curated rows.
- Template and partner blocks with pricing.
- Saved-state actions and portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Your text and images stay yours; the theme only changes the design.
