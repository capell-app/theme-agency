## What this theme gives you

A digital-awards archive look with muted greys and condensed headings. It gives your site a precise, awards-led direction for winners and projects.

## How to use it

1. Go to **Appearance > Themes** (or **Settings > Theme**).
2. Choose **Reel Room** and **Activate**.
3. Open **Theme settings** to set your logo, colours, and fonts.
4. **Preview** before making it live.

## What it adds

- Date filters, winner lists, and featured project panels.
- Jury and score explainers, credits, and technology stacks.
- Awards, media galleries, and historical links with portable demo content.

## Good to know

- Built on **Foundation Theme** - install that first.
- **Preview** to see it before visitors do.
- Your text and images stay yours; the theme only changes the design.
