## What it does for you

Events lets you publish events on your site. You create one event, set when it happens and how often it repeats, and the package builds a dated page for every occurrence. Visitors can browse the listing, see a calendar, subscribe to a feed, and RSVP when you allow it.

## Your screens

- **Events**: your list of events, with their schedule, visibility, and registration counts.
- **Event venues**: reusable places you can attach to events.
- **Event occurrences**: the individual dates the package generates from each event.
- **Event registrations**: the people who have signed up, with their status and quantity.
- **Event calendar**: an admin calendar of upcoming occurrences.

## What you can do

- Create an event with a schedule, recurrence, venue, visibility, and registration settings.
- Manage reusable venues with address and map details.
- Review and adjust the dates generated from a recurring event.
- Confirm, cancel, or review attendee registrations.
- Scan upcoming events on the admin calendar or the dashboard widget.

## Where to find it

Go to **Content > Events** in the admin to create and manage events. Venues, occurrences, registrations, and the calendar live next to it under the same area.

## Good to know

- Set an event to **Public** to show it on the site; **Unlisted** and **Private** keep it out of public listings.
- Recurring events fill in their own dates, so you do not enter each one by hand.
- Turn on **Waitlist enabled** so people can still sign up once an event is full.
