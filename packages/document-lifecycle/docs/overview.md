# Document Lifecycle

<!-- prettier-ignore-start -->

## What it does for you

Document Lifecycle manages controlled documents: things like policies and procedures that have versions, review dates, and approvals. You track each document's version, get reminded before it expires, send it for approval, and keep evidence of who accepted it.

## Your screens

- **Controlled documents**: your managed documents and their status.
- **All versions**: the version history of a document.
- **Acceptances**: who has accepted a document.

## What you can do

- Upload and version a document.
- Set review and expiry dates.
- Send a document for approval.
- Track who has accepted it.
- Archive a document when it is retired.

## Where to find it

Go to **Document Lifecycle** in the admin to manage controlled documents.

## Good to know

- Track a document's **version** and review date in one place.
- Get reminded before a document **expires**.
- Keep evidence of who accepted each version.
